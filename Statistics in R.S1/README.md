## Key Statistical Concepts

### Descriptive Statistics

Descriptive statistics summarize and describe the observed data.

Inferential statistics use sample data to estimate or test claims about a target population.

### Measures of Central Tendency

- Mean: Arithmetic average of the observations.
- Median: Middle value of an ordered dataset.
- Mode: Most frequently occurring value.

### Measures of Dispersion

- Range: Difference between the maximum and minimum values.
- IQR: Difference between the third and first quartiles.
- Variance: Average squared deviation from the mean (with the appropriate population or sample definition).
- Standard Deviation: Measure of spread around the mean.

### Percentiles and Z-Scores

- Percentiles identify positions within a distribution.
- Percentile ranks describe the relative position of a specific score.
- Z-scores express the distance from the mean in standard deviation units.

### Distribution Assessment

Histograms and Q-Q plots can help examine the shape and characteristics of a distribution.

Statistical results should always be interpreted in context, considering assumptions, uncertainty, effect size, and scientific relevance.
